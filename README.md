# Sauce & Spoon: Tablet Menu Implementation Project

This repository contains my Google Project Management Capstone Project, which focuses on the implementation of tabletop tablet menus at Sauce & Spoon, a fictional restaurant chain.

This capstone project was part of the final course in the Google Project Management Certificate on Coursera, where I took on the role of a project manager overseeing the planning, execution, and delivery of this digital transformation initiative.

---

## Project Overview

**Objective:**  
To enhance customer experience and streamline ordering processes by introducing tablet-based digital menus at all tables in Sauce & Spoon restaurants.


**Stakeholders Involved:**  
- Executive Leadership Team  
- Restaurant Managers  
- Frontline Staff  
- Technology Vendors  
- Customers

**Methodology:**  
Agile framework with iterative planning and stakeholder feedback loops.

---


## Key Deliverables

- Project Charter  
  Includes background, objectives, scope, and constraints.

- Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)  
  Tasks categorized by phases with dependencies.

- Stakeholder Register  
  Roles, responsibilities, communication methods.

- Risk Management Plan  
  Matrix identifying high/medium/low priority risks.

- Final Presentation  
  Executive summary and recommendations for future scaling.

---

## Outcome

The digital menu implementation aims to:
- Reduce wait time by 25%
- Improve order accuracy by 40%
- Increase customer satisfaction (CSAT) score by 20%
- Decrease operational workload on waitstaff
